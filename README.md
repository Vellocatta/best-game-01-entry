**ROOTBOUND — Hold the Last Garden.** A complete original garden tower-defense game: the blight comes in 15 waves, and the last garden — a single Heartseed — is all that stands between the world and the dark. Plant five kinds of defenders on a 10×6 garden, watch them grow sprout → bloom → elder as they fight, and spend the seasons: Spring doubles growth, Summer sharpens thorns and embers, Autumn pays double dew, Winter slows the pests and the plants alike. Three bosses, a boon draft after waves 3, 6, 9 and 12, procedural WebAudio sound and music, and a full title / pause / boon / victory / defeat flow. No libraries, no network, all art drawn in code.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

Or open `game/index.html` directly in a desktop browser. Keyboard: 1–5 pick a plant, click soil to plant, X sell, SPACE start a wave, P pause, F fast-forward, M mute, R restart after victory or defeat.
